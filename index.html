Research

My research lies at the intersection of molecular ecology, biodiversity science, and conservation biology. I develop and apply environmental DNA and DNA metabarcoding approaches to detect biodiversity, investigate ecological interactions, and improve monitoring of invasive and threatened species.

About Me

I am a molecular ecologist working at the intersection of biodiversity science, environmental DNA, molecular biology and conservation. My research focuses on developing and applying DNA-based approaches to understand biodiversity, species distributions and ecological interactions.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of California, Berkeley, where I work on next-generation approaches for biodiversity and biosecurity monitoring.
